Comprehensive newborn check-ups to assess feeding, weight gain, jaundice, sleep, and overall health.

Parents receive practical guidance on breastfeeding, hygiene, cord care, safe sleep, and common newborn concerns.

02Child wellness
2. Child Wellness

Regular wellness visits help monitor your child’s physical health, development, behaviour, and overall well-being.

Preventive guidance and timely screening help identify potential concerns early and support healthy childhood.

04Well baby clinic
4. Well Baby Clinic

Routine baby check-ups focus on feeding, growth, development, sleep, and age-appropriate milestones.

Parents can also discuss everyday concerns and receive guidance for keeping their baby healthy and thriving.

05Dietary counselling
5. Dietary Counselling

Personalized nutrition guidance is provided according to your child’s age, growth, eating habits, and nutritional needs.

We help parents with breastfeeding, complementary feeding, picky eating, balanced diets, and healthy meal planning.

06Nutrition assessment
6. Nutrition Assessment

A detailed assessment of height, weight, growth pattern, dietary intake, and nutritional status helps identify deficiencies or excesses.

Individual recommendations are provided to support healthy growth, immunity, and development.

07Growth and development assessment
7. Growth and Development Assessment

Regular assessment helps track height, weight, head circumference, developmental milestones, behaviour, and learning.

Early identification of growth or developmental concerns allows timely guidance, evaluation, and intervention when needed.

08Obesity / Thyroid / Diabetic Clinic
8. Obesity / Thyroid / Diabetic Clinic

Specialized evaluation and follow-up are provided for childhood obesity and common thyroid or blood-sugar-related concerns.

The focus is on appropriate screening, healthy lifestyle changes, nutrition, growth monitoring, and individualized medical management.

Meet Dr. Archana Khujat Kalyani

Dr. Archana Khujat Kalyani completed her undergraduate medical education at Bharati Vidyapeeth Medical College, Pune, followed by her postgraduate training at Smt. Kashibai Navale Medical College and General Hospital, Pune.

With over 10 years of experience in paediatrics, she is dedicated to providing compassionate, comprehensive, and evidence-based care for children from infancy through adolescence.

At Dr. Archana Khujat Kalyani Paediatric Clinic, the focus is on supporting every child’s healthy growth and development while helping parents feel informed, comfortable, and confident about their child’s healthcare.
